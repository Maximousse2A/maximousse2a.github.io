Privacy Policy

This page describes privacy considerations for the Piano learning app and this website.

Pre-launch draft. This document must be checked against the final app build before App Store publication. Unverified technical details and missing publisher information are expressly noted.

1. Publisher and contact

Publisher: Maxime Pietri. For data inquiries or privacy requests, contact maxime.pietri@icloud.com.

2. Microphone access

Note recognition requires permission to access the iPad microphone. iPadOS requests this permission and you can revoke it in Settings. Some learning features may not work without microphone access.

To verify before release: whether audio is processed entirely on device or on a server, whether audio is recorded or retained, how long it is retained, and whether audio is shared with service providers. We do not make an unverified claim that no audio is ever stored or transmitted.

3. Progress data and third-party services

The app may process lesson progress, song choices, and settings to provide its features. To verify before release: local versus cloud storage, identifiers, analytics, crash reporting, payment providers, and their respective purposes.

4. Support requests

When you voluntarily email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include, to address your request. A concrete retention period must be documented before commercial publication.

5. Visiting this website

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We have not added forms, advertising scripts, or analytics scripts to these pages. GitHub may independently process technical access information when providing hosting. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

6. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, including the GDPR where relevant, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information, and exercise other legal rights. Email maxime.pietri@icloud.com. If you are in France, you may also contact the CNIL.

7. Changes

This policy may change as the app and its service providers are finalized. The latest update date will appear at the top of this page.
