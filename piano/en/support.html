Frequently asked questions

My microphone doesn't recognize notes

Open iPadOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and allow Piano access. Keep your iPad near the instrument, reduce background noise, and check the tuning of your piano.

Do I need a MIDI keyboard?

No. Piano uses your iPad microphone to help recognize notes from an acoustic piano or compatible digital keyboard.

How do I progress through a song?

In step-by-step mode, play the requested note: the next note follows once your playing is recognized. Other modes may vary by app version.

How do I report a bug?

Include your iPad model, iPadOS version, Piano app version, steps to reproduce, and a screenshot if possible. Do not send private audio recordings unless necessary.

Billing, subscriptions, or refunds

If in-app purchases are introduced, Apple handles billing. Visit Apple Billing Support or Report a Problem to request a refund.
